# Embedded Software Architect Learning Map

Muc tieu cua workspace nay la phat trien tu ky su embedded software thanh Software Architect (SA), bat dau voi `01_MCU_RTOS`, sau do den `02_Linux_CPP`, `03_AUTOSAR`, va `04_Edge_AI`.

## Thu tu hoc de xuat

1. Bat dau voi `01_MCU_RTOS` de hoc MCU architecture, peripheral drivers, interrupts va RTOS (bo qua on tap C).
2. Hoc `02_Linux_CPP` de mo rong sang Modern C++, Linux, concurrency, testing va debugging.
3. Hoc `00_SA_Core` song song tu som; ap dung tung chu de vao lab va project MCU.
4. Di sau vao `03_AUTOSAR` neu muc tieu chinh la automotive/ECU architecture.
4. Hoc `04_Edge_AI` nhu mot nhanh chuyen sau cho intelligent edge systems.
6. Sau moi cum kien thuc, tao mot artifact trong `00_SA_Core/09_Portfolio_and_Case_Studies`: architecture diagram, ADR, interface specification, trade-off analysis, hoac prototype.

## Cach su dung moi thu muc

- `01_...`, `02_...`: thu tu uu tien hoc.
- `notes`: ghi chu bang ngon ngu cua ban.
- `references`: datasheet, standard, link va sach can doc.
- `labs`: bai tap nho de kiem chung kien thuc.
- `projects`: bai tich hop co yeu cau, thiet ke, code, test va ket luan.
- `architecture`: diagram, ADR, interface, quality attributes va trade-off.

## Dau ra can co cua mot Embedded SA

- Bien business/system requirements thanh software requirements.
- Chia component va dinh nghia interface ro rang.
- Phan tich trade-off ve latency, memory, CPU, power, safety, security va cost.
- Mo ta kien truc bang C4, UML/SysML, sequence/state/deployment diagram.
- Tao ADR va truy vet requirement -> design -> implementation -> test.
- Co kha nang review code, debug system, do performance va dan dat technical decision.

## Moc tu danh gia

- Foundation: giai thich duoc concept va lam lab rieng le.
- Practitioner: tich hop duoc mot subsystem va do duoc quality attributes.
- Architect: de xuat nhieu phuong an, bao ve trade-off, quan ly interface/risk va tao bo tai lieu kien truc hoan chinh.
